Description

Our Original Lion's Mane Tincture delivers 1000mg of premium, organic Lion's Mane mushroom extract in every bottle. This alcohol-free formula is designed for daily use to support cognitive function, memory, and mental clarity. Made by three brothers passionate about natural wellness, each batch is third-party tested for purity and potency.

Lion's Mane (Hericium erinaceus) has been used for centuries in traditional medicine for its remarkable brain-supporting properties. Our extraction process preserves the beneficial beta-glucans and hericenones that make Lion's Mane so effective. Simply take one dropper (1ml) daily, or add to your morning coffee, smoothie, or tea for an easy wellness boost.
